## 2. The 4 Highest-Converting Pin Formats to Post on Pinterest

Pinterest prioritizes **tall vertical pins (2:3 aspect ratio, 1000 × 1500 px)** with high-contrast text overlays and clear problem-solving value.

### Pin Format 1: The "3-Step Anatomy" Infographic (Highest Saves & Click-Through)
- **Layout**:
  - **Top 50%**: Close-up hero shot of the finished result (e.g. *Soft Pink Chrome Nails* or *Dewy Haldi Makeup*).
  - **Middle 35%**: 3 circular or pill thumbnails showing Step 1 (Prep), Step 2 (Base), Step 3 (Glaze/Contour).
  - **Bottom 15%**: High-contrast pill banner:
    > *"Full Step-by-Step Tutorial & Exact Products Used ➔ RoopSetu.com/beauty-guide"*
- **Target Keywords**: `pink chrome nails tutorial`, `bridal nail inspiration 2026`, `how to do chrome glaze at home`.

### Pin Format 2: "The Drugstore Dupe / Exact Products Used" Flatlay
- **Layout**:
  - Split screen: Left side shows the celebrity or bridal inspiration; Right side shows flatlay photos of the 3 affordable products used to achieve it.
  - Overlay Text: *"Bridal Lip Combo That Lasts 12 Hours (Under ₹800)"* or *"The Viral Hailey Bieber Glaze Under ₹500"*.
  - Pin Description: *"Everything you need to recreate this exact look without salon markup. Complete breakdown at RoopSetu Beauty Guide."*
- **Why it works**: Drives instant buying frenzy for affiliate clicks.

### Pin Format 3: "Bridal & Navratri Moodboard" Collage
- **Layout**:
  - 4-quadrant aesthetic collage:
    1. Bridal Lehenga / Chaniya Choli swatch
    2. HD Makeup close-up
    3. Mehendi backhand design
    4. Hair floral braid
  - Center Pill: *"Pastel Gujarati Bride Moodboard (Lookbook #04)"*.
  - Link destination: `roopsetu.com/beauty-guide` or `roopsetu.com/services?category=bridal`.
- **Why it works**: Reaches brides in the planning phase who need the look, the artist, and the attire all in one place.

### Pin Format 4: "Before vs After" 7-Second Video Pin (Idea Pin)
- **Layout**:
  - 0–2s: Bare skin or natural nails.
  - 3–5s: Brushing on the chrome glaze or blending the contour.
  - 6–7s: The stunning finished reflection in natural lighting.
  - End screen: *"Get the full recipe & find certified artists at RoopSetu"*.

## 3. Beauty Guide Page Design & Funnel Architecture

To prevent high bounce rates, the page must deliver on the pin's promise immediately:

1. **Immediate Visual Continuity**:
   - When a visitor clicks a pin for "The Viral Pink Chrome", the top of the page must prominently show that exact image and title.
2. **Interactive 3-Tab Experience**:
   - **Tab 1: Step-by-Step Breakdown**: Step 1, Step 2, Step 3 with pro tips and high-res technique photos.
   - **Tab 2: Shop The Exact Kit**: Product cards with real photos (`opi.jpg`, `beetles.jpg`, `kodi.jpg`), price tags, and direct Amazon/Nykaa buy buttons.
   - **Tab 3: Book A Verified Artist**: Local artist profile card with direct WhatsApp booking button for users who prefer in-salon or doorstep styling.
3. **Pinterest Viral Loop Button**:
   - Every look includes a prominent **"Pin This"** button (`https://pinterest.com/pin/create/button/...`) so happy visitors re-pin directly back to Pinterest, expanding your reach automatically.
